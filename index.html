01 Idea

Superintelligence
serves the individual.

Personal Informatics begins with the individual.

PIS Lab studies how people perceive, understand, anticipate, and direct intelligent systems. Through interaction design, we turn computational capability into human capability—creating products, environments, intelligent hardware, and agents that respond to individual needs and support a harmonious human–AI relationship.

  1. 01

    Perceivable

    Critical change reaches the right person at the right moment.

  2. 02

    Understandable

    Evidence, boundaries, and uncertainty form a usable mental model.

  3. 03

    Predictable

    People can inspect possible consequences before they commit.

  4. 04

    Controllable

    Questioning, steering, overriding, and recovery remain explicit.

02 Working systems

Ideas tested
in real systems.

We do not begin with a technology category. We begin with a human situation—then combine psychology, AI, design, and engineering until the whole loop works.

03 How we work

Insight emerges
through interaction.

Every project begins by inventing an interaction, puts it in front of people, builds a model from what emerges, and returns that knowledge to a product that can serve individuals better.

Example / Game interaction

A new way
to play.

Invent an interaction, put it in players' hands, use real behavior to identify the problem worth solving, then refine the experience around different players.

  1. 01Prototype a new play mechanic
  2. 02Observe how people actually play
  3. 03Model the problem that emerges
  4. 04Refine a better experience for each player
  1. 01

    Prototype

    Invent a visual, auditory, tangible, or embodied interaction, then build enough of it to test the idea.

  2. 02

    Observe

    Put people inside the prototype and study the behavior, strategies, breakdowns, and new questions that emerge.

  3. 03

    Model

    Turn observations into a working model that explains the new problem and guides the next design decision.

  4. 04

    Personalize

    Refine the prototype into a product, validate it in use, and adapt the service to individual needs.
