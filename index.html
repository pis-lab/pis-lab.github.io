01 Guiding idea

Intelligence should not
disappear inside the machine.

A system can be powerful and still leave its user behind.

PIS Lab studies the gap between what intelligent systems can do and what people can perceive, understand, anticipate, and control. We make that gap visible—then build ways to close it.

  1. 01

    Perceivable

    Critical change reaches the right person at the right moment.

  2. 02

    Understandable

    Evidence, boundaries, and uncertainty form a usable mental model.

  3. 03

    Predictable

    People can inspect possible consequences before they commit.

  4. 04

    Controllable

    Questioning, steering, overriding, and recovery remain explicit.

02 Working systems

Ideas tested
in real systems.

We do not begin with a technology category. We begin with a human situation—then combine psychology, AI, design, and engineering until the whole loop works.

03 How we work

A loop built
around the person.

Every project moves between observation and intervention. The technology changes; the human-centered loop does not.

  1. 01

    Sense

    Collect gaze, physiology, behavior, language, and context.

  2. 02

    Model

    Build an individual baseline and estimate changing cognitive state.

  3. 03

    Externalize

    Make evidence, boundaries, uncertainty, and consequences tangible.

  4. 04

    Act

    Adapt an interface, environment, training strategy, or embodied agent.

  5. 05

    Learn

    Use outcomes and human feedback to recalibrate the next cycle.

04 In the studio

Research happens
by building.

Students work across embodied robotics, immersive environments, multimodal sensing, simulation, and fabrication—moving from a question to a prototype, and from a prototype to evidence.

Our platforms are not a showroom. They are places to make uncertainty testable.

  • XR + CAVE environments
  • Embodied robotics
  • Physiological sensing
  • 3D fabrication
